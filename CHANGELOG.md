# Changelog

This file tracks changes to the proposed Open Creator Constitution. Draft updates do not constitute formal ratification.

## 0.1.1 — 2026-11-13

- Added the guiding principle to Article I: **“Human creativity establishes the destination. Artificial intelligence expands the possible routes. Evidence determines whether we've arrived.”**
- Clarified that AI-assisted exploration and implementation remain under human creative direction, authorization, and accountability, with claims of success grounded in evidence.
- Surfaced the principle in the README for prospective adopters.
- **Constitution impact:** Reinforces human authority and truthful verification. No changes to creator ownership, affordability, portability, privacy, security requirements, or licensing.
- **Approval:** Requested and authorized by the human project founder in conversation on 2026-11-13. Formal constitutional ratification remains unrecorded.
